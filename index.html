Apartments at the Braverman Building

The Braverman Building is a 16-unit apartment building at 2617 W Girard Ave in Philadelphia, built in 2016. Apartments are on floors 2 through 5. There's an elevator to every floor and a shared deck with views of the downtown skyline.

1 bedroom / 1 bath

About 750 sq ft

2 bedroom / 2 bath (corner unit)

About 856 sq ft

$1,695/month + $150/month utility charge

Every apartment has an in-unit washer and dryer, central heat and air, stainless steel appliances, and granite countertops. The utility charge covers water, sewer, trash, and common-area maintenance. Residents pay their own electricity. Rents shown are for a 12-month lease and may change. Housing vouchers are welcome.
