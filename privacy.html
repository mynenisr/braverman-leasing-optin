Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Braverman Building Leasing ("we," "us") collects and uses information when you visit this website, submit our leasing inquiry form, call our leasing number, or sign up for our leasing text messages. Braverman Building Leasing is the leasing program of Braverman Building Property Management, which manages the Braverman Building, 2617 W Girard Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19130.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your first name, mobile phone number, and the apartment type you're interested in (1 bedroom or 2 bedroom), plus anything you tell us in a call or text.
  • Text-message consent records: whether you agreed to receive leasing texts, how you agreed (web form, phone call, or texting START), the date and time, and the consent wording you saw or heard. We also keep a record if you opt out.
  • Calls to our leasing line: when you call our leasing number, our AI leasing assistant receives your phone number and what you discuss (for example, the apartment type you want and whether you'd like a showing or application link texted to you). We keep a short summary of the call for follow-up.
  • Messages: the texts we send you and the replies you send us, including STOP and HELP requests.
  • Basic technical information: like most websites, our web host may log basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running and secure. We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your leasing inquiry and follow up with you about it.
  • If you opted in, to text you showing booking links, rental application links, appointment follow-ups, and availability updates about the apartments you asked about.
  • To answer your questions, and to honor STOP and HELP requests.
  • To keep records of consent and opt-outs, and to comply with the law.

We don't sell your personal information.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our leasing communications, and only so they can provide those services to us. They include our phone and text-message provider (Twilio), our AI voice assistant provider, and our form-delivery provider, which forwards form submissions to our email. If you choose to book a showing through the scheduling link we send you, the scheduling service collects the information you enter on its site under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes; text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

Text messages

Signing up for texts is optional and is not a condition of renting or applying. Message frequency varies (about 1–4 messages per week). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms of Service for details.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry information for as long as it's needed to respond to you and follow up about your inquiry. We keep text-message consent and opt-out records for as long as you're subscribed, and afterward for as long as needed to show compliance with the law.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure.

Children

This website and our text messages are meant for adults looking for rental housing. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving texts.
  • To ask what information we have about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it, contact us using the details below. We may need to keep a record of your opt-out so we can honor it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
