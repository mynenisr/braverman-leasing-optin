Program description

Braverman Building Leasing sends leasing text messages from (445) 256-7368 to people who ask about renting an apartment at the Braverman Building, 2617 W Girard Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19130, and who agree to receive them. Messages include showing booking links, rental application links, appointment follow-ups, and availability updates about the apartments you asked about. Messages may be sent using automated technology.

How you sign up

You can sign up by checking the text-message box on our leasing inquiry form, by saying yes when our AI leasing assistant asks for permission during a call to (445) 256-7368 (see the phone opt-in script). We send a confirmation text after you sign up. Signing up is optional. Consent is not a condition of renting, applying, or seeing an apartment.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You'll usually get about 1–4 messages per week while you're looking for an apartment.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your wireless carrier for details about your plan. We don't charge you for our texts.

Opting out (STOP)

You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any of our texts. You can also reply UNSUBSCRIBE, END, QUIT, CANCEL, or STOPALL. We'll send one final text confirming you've been unsubscribed, and then you won't get any more texts from us unless you sign up again. To sign up again, reply START to any of our texts or use our web form.

Help (HELP)

Reply HELP to any of our texts for help. You can also call or text 510-353-3555 or email mynenisr@gmail.com.

Carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery depends on your carrier and device.

Your number

Please tell us if you change or give up the mobile number you signed up with, so we don't text someone else. You agree that you're the account holder or an authorized user of the number you provide.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Fair housing
