Phone opt-in script

How callers to (445) 256-7368 sign up for Braverman Building Leasing texts

Our leasing number, (445) 256-7368, is answered by an AI leasing assistant for Braverman Building Property Management. The assistant tells callers it's an AI assistant at the start of the call. It never sends texts unless the caller clearly says yes after hearing the disclosure below. Callers can also sign up on our web form.

1. The assistant asks for permission

When a caller wants a showing booking link or an application link, the assistant says:

"I can text you the link. Before I do: is it OK for Braverman Building Leasing to send leasing texts to the number you're calling from? They'd include showing and application links, appointment follow-ups, and availability updates about the apartments you asked about. It's usually about one to four messages a week, and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Saying yes isn't required to rent or apply. Our full texting terms and privacy policy are on our website, and your first text will include the link. Is that OK?"

2. The caller answers

  • If the caller says yes: the assistant confirms the mobile number, and the call record logs the consent (yes), the number, the date and time, and the script version.
  • If the caller says no, or isn't sure: no texts are sent. The assistant offers other options instead, such as calling Property Management at 510-353-3555.

3. The first text confirms the sign-up

The first text a caller receives is this confirmation:

"Braverman Building Leasing: You're signed up for leasing texts (showing/application links, follow-ups, availability updates). About 1-4 msgs/wk. Msg & data rates may apply. Help: 510-353-3555 or reply HELP. Reply STOP to opt out. Terms & privacy: https://mynenisr.github.io/braverman-leasing-optin/terms.html"

Then they get the link they asked for, for example:

"Braverman Building Leasing: Hi Alex, here's the link to book your in-person or virtual showing of a 2BR at 2617 W Girard Ave: https://lead.showdigs.com/unit/[unit-id] Questions? Call/text 510-353-3555. Reply STOP to opt out."

Opting out

Replying STOP at any time ends all texts. If a caller asks the assistant to stop contacting them, they're added to our do-not-contact list. See our SMS Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
